Step 1: Tap the green WhatsApp icon.

The button you need to tap has a yellow outline. If you tap the wrong thing, a hint will appear here.

1. Making a phone call

  1. Tap the green Phone icon 📞 on your home screen.
  2. Tap Contacts and choose the person you want to call.
  3. Tap their number. The phone will start ringing.
  4. To end the call, tap the red button.
Tip: Ask a family member to save important numbers in your contacts, so you never need to type them.

2. Sending a WhatsApp message

  1. Open WhatsApp (the green icon with a white phone).
  2. Tap the person's name in your chat list.
  3. Tap the box at the bottom that says Message and type your words.
  4. Tap the arrow ➤ to send.
  5. To send a voice message, press and hold the microphone 🎤 and speak. Let go to send.

3. Video calling your family

  1. Open WhatsApp and choose the person.
  2. Tap the camera icon 🎥 at the top of the chat.
  3. Hold the phone in front of your face so they can see you.
  4. Tap the red button to end the call.
Tip: Video calls use more internet. Use Wi-Fi at home when you can.

4. Taking and sharing photos

  1. Open the Camera app 📷.
  2. Point the phone at what you want to photograph.
  3. Tap the big round white button to take the photo.
  4. To share it, open the photo, tap Share and choose WhatsApp.
