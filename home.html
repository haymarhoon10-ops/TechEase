Welcome to TechEase

More and more everyday tasks have moved online: paying bills, booking a doctor's appointment, using government services, and even talking to family. For many older people this can feel confusing, and some are afraid of making mistakes or being scammed.

TechEase is here to help. Our guides use large text, clear pictures and simple words, with no technical jargon. You can even practise on a pretend phone before trying on your own, and make the text bigger at any time with the A+ button at the top.
