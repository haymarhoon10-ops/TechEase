🏛️ Online Services Made Easy

Do everyday tasks from home: government services, banking and appointments.

Many services you used to visit in person are now available online, 24 hours a day. Using them saves time, travel and waiting in queues.

🏛️

Government services

Renew documents, pay bills and check your requests on the official Bahrain government portal.

🏦

Online banking

Check your balance and pay bills using your bank's official app.

🩺

Appointments

Book or change appointments online instead of waiting on the phone.

1. Using government e-services

  1. Open your internet browser and type the official government website address (in Bahrain: bahrain.bh), or use the official app.
  2. Log in with your personal secure login. Never give your login details to anyone.
  3. Choose the service you need, for example paying a bill.
  4. Follow the steps on the screen and check the details before you press Submit.
  5. Save or take a photo of the confirmation number.
⚠ Always type the website address yourself. Do not click links in SMS messages that ask you to log in. They may be fake.

2. Online banking safely

  1. Download your bank's app only from the official App Store or Google Play.
  2. Log in with your password, fingerprint or face ID.
  3. Tap Accounts to see your balance.
  4. Tap Pay bills or Transfer and follow the steps.
  5. Always log out when you finish.

3. Booking an appointment

  1. Open the official app or website of the hospital, clinic or service.
  2. Choose Book appointment.
  3. Pick the date and time that suit you.
  4. Check the details and confirm. You will usually get an SMS or email confirmation.
